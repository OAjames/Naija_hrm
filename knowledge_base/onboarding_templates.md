# Onboarding Templates — NairaHR

## Standard Nigerian SME Onboarding Checklist

Use this as the base template. The agent will customise it per role and company.

---

## Documents Required from New Hire

- [ ] Signed offer letter / contract of employment
- [ ] Means of identification (NIN slip, National ID card, International Passport, or Driver's Licence)
- [ ] 2 recent passport photographs
- [ ] Academic certificates (O'Level/WAEC, B.Sc., M.Sc. as applicable)
- [ ] NYSC discharge certificate OR exemption certificate (for graduates under 30)
- [ ] Professional certifications (if role requires — e.g., ICAN, COREN, ICAN, PMP)
- [ ] Bank account details (account number, bank name, account name) for salary payment
- [ ] Pension RSA PIN (or confirmation they are opening a new RSA account)
- [ ] Tax Identification Number (TIN) — can be obtained from FIRS online
- [ ] Next of kin information (name, relationship, phone number, address)
- [ ] Medical fitness certificate (if required by role)
- [ ] Reference letters (2 professional references)

---

## Pre-Arrival (HR Actions — 1 Week Before Start Date)

- [ ] Send welcome email with start date, dress code, and reporting instructions
- [ ] Set up company email account (e.g., firstname.lastname@company.com)
- [ ] Prepare workstation/laptop and necessary equipment
- [ ] Add new hire to relevant communication channels (Slack, WhatsApp group, Teams)
- [ ] Notify IT to create system accounts (HRMS, email, internal tools)
- [ ] Inform the new hire's team lead and schedule a welcome meeting
- [ ] Prepare staff ID card (or schedule ID photo session)
- [ ] Add new hire to payroll for the month (or next payroll cycle)

---

## Day 1

### Morning
- [ ] Welcome and introduction to reception/security
- [ ] Office/facility tour (workstation, bathrooms, kitchen, exits, prayer room)
- [ ] Introduction to immediate team members
- [ ] Meet with HR: collect all onboarding documents (see list above)
- [ ] Receive employee handbook and company policy booklet

### Afternoon
- [ ] IT setup: laptop, email, internal systems access
- [ ] Introduction to company intranet/HRMS (leave portal, payslip access)
- [ ] Review of role expectations with line manager
- [ ] Confirm probation period and review schedule (typically 3–6 months)

---

## Week 1

- [ ] Read and acknowledge: Employee Handbook
- [ ] Read and acknowledge: Code of Conduct
- [ ] Read and acknowledge: Data Protection Policy (NDPA compliance)
- [ ] Read and acknowledge: IT Acceptable Use Policy
- [ ] Read and acknowledge: Leave Policy
- [ ] Attend company/department induction session (if scheduled)
- [ ] Meet with key cross-functional contacts (Finance, IT, Admin)
- [ ] Complete any mandatory e-learning or compliance training
- [ ] Shadow team members to understand workflows

---

## Month 1

- [ ] Attend 1:1 check-in with line manager (end of Week 2)
- [ ] Complete probation milestone review (if applicable)
- [ ] Submit pension RSA PIN to HR — PENCOM contribution begins
- [ ] NSITF registration confirmation filed by HR
- [ ] Confirm PAYE registration in employee's state of residence
- [ ] Group Life Insurance — add employee to company policy
- [ ] Set 90-day performance objectives with line manager
- [ ] Attend monthly all-hands or team meeting

---

## Role-Specific Additions

### Software Engineer
- [ ] Access to GitHub/GitLab organisation
- [ ] Dev environment setup guide provided
- [ ] Code review standards document
- [ ] Introduction to engineering team processes (sprints, standups)

### Sales Executive
- [ ] Territory assignment and briefing
- [ ] CRM system access and training
- [ ] Product/service training sessions scheduled
- [ ] Introduction to existing key clients (shadowing)

### Product Manager
- [ ] Access to product roadmap tools (Jira, Notion, Figma)
- [ ] Review of current product strategy and OKRs
- [ ] Stakeholder mapping sessions
- [ ] Introduction to engineering and design counterparts

### Finance / Accounts
- [ ] Access to accounting software (Sage, QuickBooks, Xero)
- [ ] Banking mandate inclusion (if role requires)
- [ ] Compliance training: FIRS filing calendar
- [ ] Introduction to external auditors/consultants
