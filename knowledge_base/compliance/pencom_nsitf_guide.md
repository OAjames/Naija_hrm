# Pension & NSITF Compliance Guide (Nigeria)

> For Nigerian SME HR teams. Sources: Pension Reform Act 2014, NSITF Act.

---

## PENCOM — Pension Contributions

### Who Must Comply
- All employers with **3 or more employees** in Nigeria.
- Both private and public sector organisations.

### Contribution Rates
| Party | Rate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Employer | **10%** | Monthly emoluments |
| Employee | **8%** | Monthly emoluments |
| **Total** | **18%** | Per employee per month |

**Monthly emoluments** = basic salary + housing allowance + transport allowance.  
Excludes: overtime, annual bonuses (unless contractually recurring).

### Process for HR
1. Employees choose their Pension Fund Administrator (PFA) from PENCOM's approved list.
2. HR collects the employee's Retirement Savings Account (RSA) PIN.
3. Deduct 8% from employee's pay.
4. Add employer's 10%.
5. Remit total 18% to the employee's PFA **within 7 working days** of salary payment.
6. Late remittance penalty: 2% per month of the unremitted amount.

### New Employee Onboarding Step
- New hire must open an RSA account with a PENCOM-licensed PFA if they don't have one.
- Collect RSA PIN from employee and record in HR system.
- First remittance must be made by the end of the first month of employment.

### Common Questions

**Q: My employee already has an RSA from a previous employer. What do I do?**  
A: Collect their existing RSA PIN. Contributions simply continue into the same account. No new account is needed.

**Q: What happens if we miss a remittance?**  
A: PENCOM charges 2% per month on the outstanding amount. Repeated non-compliance can result in prosecution. Remit as soon as possible and document the delay.

**Q: Are contract/temporary staff covered?**  
A: Employees on contracts of 3 months or more are covered by the Pension Reform Act and must be enrolled.

## NSITF — National Social Insurance Trust Fund

### What It Is
NSITF provides compensation for employees who suffer workplace accidents, occupational diseases, or work-related deaths.

### Contribution Rate
- **1% of total monthly payroll** paid entirely by the employer.
- Employees make no NSITF contribution.

### Who Must Register
- All employers operating in Nigeria, regardless of size.
- Registration must happen **within 6 months** of starting business or at first hire.

### Process for HR
1. Register the company at the nearest NSITF office or via nsitf.gov.ng.
2. Obtain an NSITF Employer Registration Number.
3. Calculate 1% of your total monthly payroll (sum of all employee emoluments).
4. Remit by the end of each month.
5. Keep proof of remittance — required for regulatory audits and government contract bids.

### NSITF and Employee Benefits
- If an employee is injured at work, they may claim compensation from NSITF.
- Compensation covers: medical expenses, temporary or permanent disability payments, survivor benefits for work-related deaths.
- HR must report workplace accidents to NSITF within **7 days** of the incident.

### Common Questions

**Q: We are a small startup with 2 employees. Do we need to register for NSITF?**  
A: Yes. NSITF registration is mandatory for all employers, regardless of company size.

**Q: An employee was injured on the way to work. Does NSITF cover this?**  
A: Generally, NSITF covers injuries that occur during work or while performing work-related duties. Commuting accidents may or may not be covered depending on circumstances. Report to NSITF within 7 days and let them assess.

**Q: How do we calculate the 1% for a month where we paid bonuses?**  
A: Include all cash payments made to employees in that month, including bonuses, in the payroll total. Calculate 1% of the full amount.

## Group Life Insurance (Pension Reform Act 2014)

- Employers must maintain a **Group Life Insurance** policy for all employees.
- Minimum coverage: **3× the employee's annual total emoluments**.
- Premiums are paid entirely by the employer.
- This is distinct from health insurance (which is not yet mandatory).

## ITF — Industrial Training Fund

### Who Must Pay
- Companies with **5 or more employees**, OR
- Companies with an annual turnover of **₦50 million or more**.

### Contribution
- **1% of total annual payroll**.
- Remit by **1 April** of each year for the prior year.

### Benefit
- Companies can reclaim up to 60% of their ITF contribution as reimbursement for training costs, if they submit approved training reports.

## Compliance Checklist for New Company Setup

- [ ] Register company with Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
- [ ] Register with Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) for PAYE
- [ ] Register with PENCOM and select a PFA for contributions
- [ ] Register with NSITF — obtain Employer Registration Number
- [ ] Register with ITF (if applicable)
- [ ] Obtain Group Life Insurance policy
- [ ] Set up payroll system to calculate and track all statutory deductions
